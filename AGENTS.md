# Working in this repository

The vault remains the project's thinking space. Ted has now authorised review/integration of the accepted EPUB experiments and one limited milestone for reusable PRIME and authored-chat components. See `vault/Managed EPUB Foundation.md` and `vault/Decisions.md`. This supersedes the initial no-tickets-yet direction only for that scope; it does not authorise a general platform backlog or implementation of the new milestone in the planning task.

- Read `vault/Home.md`, `vault/Decisions.md` and the relevant notes before making changes.
- Preserve the distinction between Ted's stated intentions, agreed directions, proposed designs and unanswered questions.
- Capture and develop ideas before creating tickets. Do not generate a backlog, scaffold an application, deploy a site or choose a technology stack unless requested.
- Obsidian is the current authoring workshop, not a permanent dependency or settled platform choice.
- Preserve readable Markdown, relative asset paths and Obsidian links. Avoid unnecessary plugin dependencies and machine-specific workspace state.
- Keep manuscript wording intact when working with extracts. Clearly label adaptations and experiments. Use the current authoritative manuscript when adapting; do not assume an old ZIP is current.
- Preserve authored dialogue. Timing and presentation are editorial metadata; they must not silently change the words.
- Do not introduce live generative character dialogue by default. An authored chat sequence is a playback format, not a chatbot.
- Do not turn conceptual references such as The Sandman and Watchmen into assets or adaptations without an explicit request and appropriate rights.
- Keep all claims about prototypes, hosting, payment and reader demand tied to evidence. A proposed feature is not an implemented feature.
- Keep the vault in this repository as it develops; do not create a competing canonical copy elsewhere.
- When reviewing a Story Teller publication or Supporting Content changes, apply the point-of-access editorial rule in `vault/Supporting Content.md`: inspect previews, full notes and all onward links against the reader's earliest access, flagging spoilers and assumed knowledge. Book-local scope alone does not establish spoiler safety; do not claim an automatic spoiler filter exists.
