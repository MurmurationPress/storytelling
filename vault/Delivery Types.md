# Delivery Types

These are Ted's initial five delivery forms. The controls below are design possibilities, not a finished specification.

| Delivery | Possible editorial controls | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prose | Text, layout, page breaks, optional narration | How do pages reflow at different text sizes? |
| Comic/image frames | Asset, caption, composition, frame order, reveal | When is a frame fixed, responsive or zoomable? |
| Chat sequences | Speaker, message order, typing state, pauses, reveal style | What advances playback and how does a reader skip or replay? |
| Videos | Poster, captions, playback cue, return position | When should the reader choose to play? |
| Interactive maps | Initial view, layers, locations, staged reveals | Which interactions contribute to the story? |

## Content, delivery and advancement

A proposed model separates three editorial decisions:

1. What content is being presented?
2. How is it delivered?
3. What advances the sequence: page turn, tap, elapsed time or interaction?

This is a way to think about the format, not an agreed schema. One content source may support multiple treatments, but prose, comic and audio adaptations may require editorial work rather than automatic conversion.
