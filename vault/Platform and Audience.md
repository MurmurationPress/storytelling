# Platform and Audience

## Beyond a single adaptation

Ted's wider idea is a platform for this type of mixed-media publication. He suggested imagining works such as The Sandman or Watchmen reinterpreted in this medium. They are conceptual reference points; no adaptation or asset reuse is proposed here.

PRIME could be the first real work through which to discover the platform's needs. A working example could also demonstrate human/AI collaboration through an actual publication experience.

## Ghost and independent hosting

Ghost was initially considered as a possible home for an embedded reader, membership and paid access. Ted then explicitly opened the possibility of building an independent site. Ghost is neither required nor ruled out.

No hosting, payment, membership or technical integration choice is made. The available Sites capability is a possible prototyping route, not a commitment to a long-term hosting architecture.

## An article as a call to action

The musings idea **The Book I Originally Wanted to Write** has become a proposal to readers: would they want this experience, and would they pay for it?

Source: [musings Ideas.md](https://github.com/tedwarddick/musings/blob/main/Ideas.md). That article plan remains in musings; these notes develop the related platform idea rather than replacing the article's home.

Describe the experience concretely. A prototype could let people judge it before answering. Interest, willingness to pay and actual purchasing are different evidence.

The platform also raises a second audience question: would other authors or publishers want to create work this way? Do not assume that reader enthusiasm establishes demand for authoring software.

Pricing, subscriptions, ownership, offline access and rights remain open. No market research or commercial validation has been performed.
