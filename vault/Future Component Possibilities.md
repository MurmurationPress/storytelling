# Future Component Possibilities

These remain vault possibilities, outside the limited [[Managed EPUB Foundation]] milestone. No tickets are created for them in this stage. Ted's original [[Component Types]] is preserved byte-for-byte; this note adds scope context without rewriting his list.

- Skip's performed drawing: presentation unfolding as authored action, grounded in the actual passage before specifying a component.
- An invitation to continue a line: reader participation is a distinct idea from watching a drawing; its narrative role remains open.
- JANUS: sophisticated reports/triples and perhaps charts; not yet a selected treatment or necessarily a single component. See [[PRIME and JANUS]].
- Contextual/hover previews: local previews with bounded return, considering touch rather than assuming hover exists everywhere.
- Video: poster/playback, captions, narrative role and offline/portable asset treatment remain questions.
- Applications, including a murmuration application: local interaction must coexist with reader-owned sequence/navigation.
- Footnotes: optional material, bounded aside and return/state restoration.
- Sound/music: Ted imagines an analogue-modem quality for some PRIME output; sound is an open editorial option, not a requirement of the accepted silent typing treatment.

Do not choose a general component catalogue or authoring schema from these possibilities. Revisit a possibility when a real passage and reading purpose justify an experiment.
