# Conversation Record — 20 October 2026

This is a summary of the visible discussion, not a verbatim transcript. It preserves attribution where it matters.

## Article and reader interest

Ted clarified that Ideas.md is in the root of the musings repository. Three ideas were added there: Intelligence Needs a Habitat; When Your Companion Belongs to Someone Else; The Book I Originally Wanted to Write.

Ted explained that the last idea is a call to action: are people interested, including in paying? An embedded reader might fit Ghost, but the world lends itself to a wiki more than a traditional page. The musings note was updated to reflect that purpose.

## Wider platform

Ted proposed a mixed-media storytelling platform and cited The Sandman and Watchmen as imaginative examples. He sees a new interpretation as compatible with book sales, using the analogy of books and films.

He suggested trying a prototype to demonstrate human/AI collaboration. EMERGENCE and CONSTRAINT were considered; Companions made CONSTRAINT particularly interesting. He subsequently identified Art as a good starting chapter.

Ted also pointed out that ChatGPT can build sites, so Ghost need not constrain the experiment.

## Delivery and authoring

Ted described chat sequences unfolding as if people were typing, with messages arriving one at a time and sometimes only three wiggling dots visible.

He proposed Obsidian as an authoring tool with markup for interaction types and timings, and a storyboard app offering prose, comic/image frames, chat sequences, video and interactive maps.

For PRIME/JANUS dialogue, Ted proposed PRIME text arriving like teletext or an old typewriter, with JANUS output more fluid and sophisticated. He explicitly said that solving this need not be part of Art.

## The trigger and the immediate request

Ted said that the idea resurfaced when listening to the book on Eleven: the narrator's handling of a callout was awful.

He requested a repository to record ideas, with the vault inside the repository. Obsidian is familiar and extensible, but may not be the long-term authoring platform. The vault should allow ideas to develop before launching into tickets, buying thinking time.

## Assistant proposals retained as possibilities

Reusable presentation profiles; separation of content, delivery and advancement; reader controls for timed sequences; optional linked depth; progress-aware revelation; an independent browser prototype; a small demonstration before payment or account infrastructure.
