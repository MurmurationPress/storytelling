# The Last Lantern

Mara found the old radio beneath the ferry seat. Its dial glowed despite the missing battery.

---

```chat
Mara: Is anyone still at the lighthouse?
Ivo: Only me. The stairs have gone quiet.
Mara: Keep the lantern lit. I'm coming across.
```

---

The ferry touched the jetty. Mara waited for the radio to speak again.

```chat
Ivo: There's a second light out on the water.
Mara: That's my boat.
Ivo: It isn't moving with you.
```

She turned. Beyond the wake, a small amber light held perfectly still.
